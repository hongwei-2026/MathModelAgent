# Style card: MCM/ICM (English)

Distilled structure only — do not paste full award PDFs into context.

## Skeleton

1. Summary Sheet (one page; control number; restated problem; approach; **key numbers**; strengths)
2. Contents (if template requires)
3. Introduction / Restatement
4. Assumptions & Justifications
5. Notations
6. Model Design (by part A/B/C as needed)
7. Solution & Results (tables/figures with discussion)
8. Sensitivity / Uncertainty
9. Strengths & Weaknesses
10. Conclusions
11. References + short appendix (no code dump)

## Summary rhythm

- Problem in 2–3 sentences
- Modeling approach chain
- **Quantitative** headline results
- Limitations one sentence

## Density

- One primary result table per major part
- At most one overview flowchart per section
- Captions below figures; discuss after each figure

## Do not learn

- Filler pages of code
- Summary without numbers
- Claiming MCM “O award style” without evidence
- Internal workflow filenames in the body
